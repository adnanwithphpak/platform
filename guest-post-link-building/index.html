A service built around link portfolio planning

Guest Post Platform connects sites competing in organic search with publication opportunities selected around topic relevance, natural anchors and sustainable pacing. Each campaign starts with the target page, audience, market and acceptable risk level—not a random list of domains.

Authority scores and estimated traffic are useful filters, but they are not guarantees. We also review topical history, editorial consistency, indexation signals, outbound-link patterns and whether the proposed article would genuinely help the publication’s readers.

What the workflow includes

  1. Campaign brief: target URLs, topics, audience, market and budget.
  2. Publisher shortlist: relevant options with available quality indicators and pricing.
  3. Approval: you review the proposed direction before content or placement proceeds.
  4. Editorial content: an original article shaped for the publisher and its readers.
  5. Publication reporting: live URL, agreed anchor and target-page confirmation.

How we review opportunities

We assess relevance first, then use authority and traffic indicators as supporting evidence. We check recent content, topic consistency, apparent audience, editorial presentation and obvious spam signals. Final editorial control always remains with the independent publisher.
