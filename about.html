About us

We vet every site so you don't have to

GuestPostPlatform was built to fix a simple problem in link building: most "guest post" marketplaces sell placements on low-quality, barely-indexed sites with inflated metrics.

What we actually check before listing a site

Every publisher in our inventory is manually reviewed for real organic traffic, editorial standards, and consistent indexing — not just a Domain Authority number pulled from a tool.

  • Traffic verified via independent SEO tools
  • Manual check for spam / PBN patterns
  • Confirmed do-follow, non-sponsored-tagged links
  • History of indexing new content within days

Our process

  1. Publisher vetting

    Sites are checked for traffic, spam signals and editorial quality before being added to inventory.

  2. Client matching

    We match your niche and budget to the right DA tier and publication type.

  3. Content & publishing

    Content is reviewed for the publication's guidelines before it goes live.

  4. Reporting

    You get the live URL and an indexing check after publication.
