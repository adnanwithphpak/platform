Quality framework

Five checks before a publisher reaches your shortlist

A placement is reviewed as an editorial environment, not reduced to one score.

Topical fit

The site covers campaign planning with enough depth to support a natural article.

Traffic pattern

We look for relevant ranking pages and stable visibility—not an impressive total with no topical overlap.

Editorial history

Recent posts, real authorship, readable pages and consistent subject coverage are reviewed manually.

Link hygiene

Excessive sponsored posts, unrelated outbound links and obvious network footprints are rejection signals.

Content standard

The proposed article must satisfy the publication while supporting your target page naturally.

What we will not promise

We do not guarantee a number-one ranking, referral sales or permanent publisher ownership. Search performance depends on your technical foundation, content quality, competition and the full link profile.

We also do not substitute an unapproved domain, hide the destination URL or describe a placement as editorial when it is not. If a publication changes its requirements, you receive the choice to approve the change.

For managed guest posting service, the goal is a defensible link that is relevant in context and useful as part of a broader SEO strategy.

Frequently asked questions

How do you select publishers for managed guest posting service?+

We start with topical and audience relevance, then review organic visibility, recent publishing activity, editorial consistency and outbound-link patterns. Clients review placement options while we handle research, outreach, writing and publication follow-up.

Can I approve a site before the campaign is written?+

Yes. We share the proposed publication and available quality information before content production. You can decline an option that does not fit the brief.

Do you guarantee rankings or a specific Domain Authority?+

No responsible provider can guarantee rankings. Third-party authority scores are useful filters, but relevance, real search visibility, editorial quality and the strength of your own page also matter.

What information should I send to get an accurate plan?+

Share your target pages, preferred markets, topics to avoid and current link-building activity. For brands and agencies that want placements managed end to end, these details help us recommend safer anchors and more relevant publishers.
