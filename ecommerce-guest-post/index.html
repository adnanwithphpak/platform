What makes a strong ecommerce placement?

A useful placement is published where the audience and editorial context match your subject. For eCommerce, our research can cover retail, DTC, marketplaces and conversion strategy. The main review priority is commercial relevance without promotional filler, supported by authority indicators, estimated traffic and publication history.

The article should stand on its own for readers. A contextual backlink is planned only where it naturally supports the discussion and the target page. Typical campaign targets include category, guide and platform pages.

Our publisher vetting checklist

  • Regular, substantive coverage of the relevant topic—not an isolated category page.
  • Clear editorial presentation and content written for a recognizable audience.
  • Organic visibility and indexation signals considered alongside third-party metrics.
  • No obvious pattern of unrelated, thin or high-risk outbound links.
  • A realistic topic, anchor and target-page relationship.

Campaign process

Share your target URL, preferred market, audience and budget. We research possible publishers, provide options for review, coordinate original content and report the live placement. Publisher availability and turnaround vary, so both are confirmed before work begins.

Yes. We can present available options for approval before content production.
